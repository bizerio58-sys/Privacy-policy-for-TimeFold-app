# Privacy-policy-for-TimeFold-app
Privacy Policy
Effective Date: Nov 24
App Name: TimeFold
Overview
TimeFold ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how TimeFold handles your information when you use our mobile application.
1. Data Collection & Storage
•
Local-Only Storage: TimeFold is an offline-only application. All commitments, scenarios, and user preferences you create are stored exclusively in local storage on your device.
•
No Cloud Transmission: We do not collect, transmit, sync, or store any personal data on external servers or cloud databases.
2. Personal Information
•
We do not collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.
•
No user accounts or logins are required to use TimeFold.
3. Third-Party Services & Analytics
•
TimeFold contains no third-party SDKs, trackers, analytics tools, or advertising networks.
•
Your data never leaves your device and is never shared with third parties.
4. Permissions
•
TimeFold operates with minimal system permissions required strictly for local app execution and edge-to-edge display rendering.
5. Children's Privacy
•
TimeFold does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected from any users, the app is safe for users of all ages.
6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
•
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app update notes.
